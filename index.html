01 / About Me

Curious about the story behind the numbers.

A practical, data-driven approach to financial analysis, business research, and corporate decision-making.

I am pursuing my MBA specializing in Finance and Business Analytics at Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana. My core interests lie in financial statement analysis, corporate finance, Power BI dashboarding, and business statistics.

During my Finance Internship at Deal Squard, I conducted comparative financial statement analysis of leading Indian online travel portals (MakeMyTrip, EaseMyTrip, Yatra), evaluating profitability, liquidity, and operational efficiency.

Prior to my post-graduation, I worked as a Key Account Manager at SD Global Logistics, overseeing major accounts and ensuring 95%+ on-time deliveries. I aim to leverage this combination of operational understanding and financial analytical skills in dynamic business roles.
